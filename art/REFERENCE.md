# Kid Goku reference sheet

Turtle School gi, as in the 21st World Martial Arts Tournament through Baba (anime) and Tenkaichi 3's Kid Goku
(looked at for proportion and color only).

## Proportions
- About 3 heads tall, Mario's height. The head is wide and round, with a small chin. The hair adds half a head on top
  and spreads wider than the shoulders.
- Short limbs, big hands and feet for an N64 read.

## Hair (black, about 220 tris)
- Front: 3 bangs over the forehead, the middle one longest, plus 1 short spike over each eye.
- Top and back: 5 big spikes fanned out like a palm (1 straight up, 2 up-and-out, 2 back-and-down).
- Sides: 1 spike above each ear sweeping back. No sideburns.

## Face (texture)
- Big round black eyes with one white highlight, thick short brows, a small mouth. Blinks like Mario (open, half,
  closed).

## Outfit
- Gi top: red-orange, short sleeves to mid upper arm, crossed collar with the left side over the right, no undershirt.
- 亀 kanji: black on a cream circle, a large one on the back and a small one on the left chest.
- Belt: dark navy, knotted at the front with two short tails.
- Pants: red-orange and baggy, tucked at the ankle.
- Wristbands: dark navy. Shoes: dark navy kung fu slip-ons.
- Tail: brown, from the lower back, curling up.
- Power Pole: red-brown staff slung diagonally across his back, top end over the right shoulder.
